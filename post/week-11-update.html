Week 11 Update

By Hannah Saturday, July 25, 2026 Built with: Faith Church - Missouri
Hey guys! House number 9 is finished! One more house built and one more family impacted this week! We picked the team up on Saturday afternoon, this group was from Faith Church located in Missouri. We took the team to hike Volcano Pacaya on Sunday and then we started the build on Monday. We finished building on Wednesday and dedicated the house. This week we built for a single mother named Olga (42), her son David (15) and daughter Isabel (8). It was so great seeing all the kids from the community come over to our worksite to color and play with the kids we were building for as well as the kids from our team this week. It was such a blessing getting to know people from this team and also getting to work with Nick this week! On Thursday we went to the Liberty Christian girls school and the team brought donations for the kids and we stayed for recess! After that we got lunch at La Belle Vie. On Friday we took the group to Antigua, the road was blocked for a while so we stopped somewhere for lunch and we were still able to make it to Antigua for a couple hours before coming back to San Raymundo! We had 4 birthdays for this group this week and it was so sweet getting to celebrate with them! We took the team back to the airport this morning and got to rest the remainder of the day. We pick our last teams up tomorrow from the airport. Please pray for this week’s home builds and all the groups we will be working with! Thank you all for following along! Hasta Luego!
