Made it to San Raymundo

By Hannah Sunday, May 10, 2026
Sorry for the delayed update! This morning we went to a local church called 'Aposento Alto' which means 'Upper Room'. After service, we stopped at a coffee shop and were shown around downtown where we can stop for groceries.
