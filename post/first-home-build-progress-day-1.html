First Home Build Progress - Day 1

By Hannah Wednesday, May 13, 2026
Hey guys! So we started building the first home today. The first day of a build here consists of making sure the land is flat and big enough for the home design. Then, we set up the framing boards to prepare for the cement pouring. In Guatemala, there are not cement trucks so they use smaller mixers here to create the cement in smaller batches and pour it. We also put up the 3 posts at the front of the home and this will be where the porch and front door are located. Attached are some photos from today. The picture of the stairs and the truck are showing the path we took to get to the build site. I've been told the home design in Guatemala is simpler, but the path to the job site is more difficult. Thanks for following along!
