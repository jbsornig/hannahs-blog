Week 8 Update

By Hannah Saturday, July 4, 2026 Built with: Mount Pulaski Christian Church - Illinois
Happy 4th of July!! What a week! The group this week was from Mount Pulaski, Illinois and they were so great! Kelsey and I were able to go on a walk with Vallery Sunday afternoon to her dad's property and it was so beautiful! Then, we picked our team up Sunday night and started building on Monday. The family we were building for this week was Eduardo (36) who is a farmer, his wife Irma (27), and their children Edwin (8), Rudy (11), Dylan (4), Yensi (1), and their 3 month old baby who did not yet have a name. The property we were building on was surrounded by their other family members homes so it was really cool to see their extended family interact with the team this week as well. The Pastor for this family is Diego Morales and he was so kind and helpful throughout the build. It was such a blessing seeing all the kids interact with our group as well as the family and Pastor speaking with our team. It was really great to see how much they opened up to our group after just a couple days! After finish the house on Wednesday, we went to another school around this area called Morning Glory and the team did crafts with two classrooms on Thursday morning and in the afternoon we went to the Mayan ruins. On Friday, we took the team to Antigua and I was able to go inside Iglesia de la Merced with some of my group and it was so beautiful to see. After that, we ate at Ta'cool and did some shopping. It was so great getting to know everyone in this group and bonding with them over the love of Jesus. Thanks for following along everybody! We are having a little Fourth of July celebration here tonight where we will have hotdogs and fireworks! Love and miss you guys!
