Week 4 Progress

By Hannah Saturday, June 6, 2026 Built with: M9ThirtyEight
¿Qué pasa calabaza? Hey guys! It's been such a good week! The group I had this week was a family from Illinois and they were so fun! The team arrived on Sunday and got the roof on the house by Tuesday! On Wednesday, we installed the electrical, door, windows and trim and did the dedication. The family members we built for this week were Felipe (56), Manuela (72), their daughter Maria (34) and her son Luis (13). It was so sweet seeing how the kids in this group played with the neighborhood children that visited the home we were building. We got ice cream every night with our group and coffee from this restaurant called Gato's. On Thursday, we stopped at Liberty Christian Academy and got a tour from Wilmer's wife, Valerie, and then we took the group to see the Mayan ruins and we were able to catch some amazing views of the mountains! Then on Friday we went to Antigua and ate at a BBQ place called Pappy's with the team. I had so much fun talking with everyone in this team and learning how to play some card games: trash and taco, cat, goat, cheese, pizza! It was bittersweet saying goodbye to this group today and I thank God for allowing me to be part of their mission trip this past week. I will try to post more frequently!! Thank you so much for the prayers and love!!
