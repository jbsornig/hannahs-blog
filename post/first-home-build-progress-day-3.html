First Home Build Progress - Day 3

By Hannah Friday, May 15, 2026
Hey guys! Officially one week in Guatemala today and we finished the first home build today as well! This is my first home build ever and the people training me were kind enough to give me the Casas plate that gets placed on the street of a family that has been approved for a home build with this organization. It is a sign of hope for them and once the home is built, the plate is removed. Today we finished the home and learned how to do some electrical as well. After the home was complete, the dedication began. The dedication is where the local pastor comes to the home and we sing, read from the Bible and speak to the family about God. This home was for a single mom named Blanca and her 8 year old son named Elvis. Attached are a few photos from today. Thank you so much for your continued prayers and words of encouragement, you have no idea how much they are appreciated!!
