First Home Build Progress - Day 2

By Hannah Thursday, May 14, 2026
Today we took the framing boards off of the cement and made all of the walls for the home as well as the rafters. Before leaving for the day we also started on the siding. There will be 3 rooms in the home when it is complete. Who knew hammering nails could be so difficult? Everyone I've worked with has been so kind and helpful. Attached are some photos of the current status of the home and some pictures of downtown from today. Thank you for the continued prayers and support!
