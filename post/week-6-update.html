Week 6 Update

By Hannah Sunday, June 21, 2026
Hey guys, last week most of the interns didn't have a build so we were in the office working on hanging sound panels for the main building for Casas in San Raymundo and then we painted the majority of another building for Casas here. It was a nice week to get to know the other interns more and also reset. We played a lot of soccer this week and also went swimming on Friday at these pools they have about an hour away from where we are staying. It was a really good week! One of the interns did have a group and a build last week and one of the team members of that group was a full time Casas staff when Cassie and Nathan were interns in 2016! So that was a really cool connection! Kelsey and I picked up our team today for this week's build so stay tuned for updates on that. Thanks for following along and for all the prayers and encouragement. It really is so uplifting for me. Love you guys <3
