Week 3 Build Progress

By Hannah Saturday, May 30, 2026 Built with: New Center Baptist Church - Hartselle, AL
Happy Saturday! It has been a busy week. We welcomed our first team on Monday from New Center Baptist Church in Hartselle, Alabama. We built a home for a family of 8 this week! The parents are Victoriano and Maria and their children are Victor (17), Brenda (14), Anyeli (12), Yesica (10), Mynor (8) and Nataly (6). The group we had this week has been building with Casas for 15 years and they were so awesome to work with! Because of the size of the property for this home we had to modify the house and remove the porch. The team brought decorations for the home to hang up when it was complete! It was amazing seeing the love of God this week in this group and the family we were building for. The team also had a VBS prepared for after building on Wednesday and Thursday so I have attached some of those photos too. Yesterday we stopped at a local school called Liberty Christian Academy which is run by Molly, the wife of Heyson who is one of the Casas Missionaries here in Guatemala. This is a school for girls and it is incredible what they are doing for the community. They are currently working on expanding the school, each year they add a new grade for their most senior class of students. They are working to build more classrooms for the coming years and it is so cool to see! I have attached some photos from the school and a brochure with some more information. After visiting the school, we took our team to Antigua for a fun day of food and shopping. Erin and I decided on McDonalds for lunch and then she showed me the famous Catholic church, Iglesia de la Merced. After that we met back up with our group and did some shopping. This morning we ate breakfast with the group and sent them off to the airport. We will go tomorrow to pick up our new group for the week! Stay tuned and thank you so much for your encouragement and prayers - I really appreciate all of it!
