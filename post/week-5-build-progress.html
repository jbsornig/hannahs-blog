Week 5 Build Progress

By Hannah Sunday, June 14, 2026 Built with: Beecher City Church of Christ
¿Qué hiciste hoy? Hey guys, another week has flown by and another house was built with an awesome group from Illinois! So many great people in this group and wow did they work so hard! Started building Monday and was done by early Wednesday afternoon. This home was built for Bacilio (63), Eustaquia (63), and their daughter, Maria (37). Bacilio is a corn and bean farmer. Him and his wife have 10 children total. Saturday was Bacilio’s birthday so this group decided to get him a tres leches cake and some trick candles to celebrate after the dedication on Wednesday! It was so fun getting to know all the people involved in the build this week. On Thursday we stopped at the home this team built two years ago and got to speak to the owners. There was some farm land behind their house so we explored a little before leaving and it was beautiful! On Friday we took the team to Antigua for the day and then we left from there to drop them off at the airport for their flight home. It was such a great week and so awesome seeing God in how the neighborhood children were interacting with the team and how the people on the team really wanted to connect with the family and the community in the short time we had with them. What a blessing. Thank you Lord for another week! I got to hike a volcano this week too so I will be posting pictures from that next! Love you guys!
