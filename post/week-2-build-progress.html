Week 2 Build Progress

By Hannah Thursday, May 21, 2026 Built with: Intern Training Program
Hey guys! It's been a busy week! We built a home for a 74 year old woman named Dominga. She has been widowed for over a decade now and lives with her daughter in law, Celi, and granddaughter, Graciela. The property for this home was way up on a hill so bringing everything up and back down was a task! Haha. The location was in a village called Zarzal in San Raymundo, but it was about an hour from where we are staying. On Monday we got the form set, poured concrete and placed the posts for the porch. On Tuesday we got all the walls up, made and placed the rafters, started the purlins and began the siding. On Wednesday we finished the siding, the roof, plywood, door and windows and the trim. Yesterday we also did the dedication for the home! And afterwards they took us to their local church and it was beautiful! Today, we went back to be trained on the electrical work and review all the wiring needed for each home. I've attached some photos from this week. The builds this week and last week were done with the Guatemalan team they hire to help with difficult builds or with groups that need more workers to complete the home and they were so fun!! Definitely a great group of guys to work with!
