Week 7 Update

By Hannah Saturday, June 27, 2026 Built with: Trinity Church Opelika
Hey guys! This week we had Trinity Church youth group from Opelika, Alabama come and build a house with us! The missionary leading this group was Gus and he is full time in the Texas location for Casas. This was his home church so that was really awesome to get to be part of this group for the week and get to know everyone! We picked the group up on Sunday from the airport in Guatemala City and started building on Monday, we finished the home and dedicated on Wednesday. The family we were building for this week was Magna (66) who is a widow, her grandson, German (11) and granddaughter, Magna Nikte (4). It was so cool to see how this group worked so hard to finish this home and everyone was so willing to help in any way they could. There were a lot of neighborhood kids that came to see this home being built and to play with the kids from this group. What a blessing to see how God was working this week in the home build and also in the kids playing with the young adults from this group. Everyone was so sweet and easy to talk to. On Thursday, I was able to witness three of these young adults get baptized! Then we went to lunch at Wilmer's father's restaurant, La Belle Vie. From there, we walked to the soccer field and whoever didn't play soccer we took to the market and the downtown area of San Raymundo. On Friday, we went to Antigua for the day and Kelsey and I were able to tag along with the girls from this group, Regan was the leader and she was so great! We rode tuc-tuc's up to see the cross over the city. Overall this week was so fun and uplifting. Every night before devotional the young adults led worship which was just so wonderful to be part of. Love you all and thank you for the prayers & encouragement.
