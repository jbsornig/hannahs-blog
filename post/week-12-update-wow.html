Week 12 Update - WOW

By Hannah Friday, July 31, 2026 Built with: University Baptist Fort Worth, TX
Alright guys, I can't believe this is the last post about my last build of the summer here. What an amazing week! The team I had this week was so incredible and kind. I could not have asked for a better group to end the summer with. I got to work with Erin again this week which was also such a blessing, she has been such an incredible friend and joy to work with this summer. The family we built for this week was a single mother, Reyna, and her 7 year old son, Angel. The team we had was so eager to help and it was really cool to see the family dynamics this week because we had so many parents with their children on this build. I was also blessed to get to work with one of the local Guatemalans here in San Raymundo named Eduardo - he drove me and Erin everyday and stayed to build with us and we could not have done it without him. The dedication this week was super special, our team bought decorations for the home and a few of our team members spoke fluent Spanish so it was super sweet getting to see them connect with the family and also translate during the dedication. Reyna's father helped with so much of the build and his kind words during the dedication will be something I will never forget. Ahhh I can't believe it's the end of this beautiful summer in San Raymundo. I am so grateful for this experience and the love and support from all of you guys!! I have no words for how much I saw God move this summer and the growth and new friendships I've experienced. I am excited to get back to the States to see my friends and family, but I know it will be hard to say goodbye to this place and these people. Thank you all again for following along with me and cheering me on and all the prayers! I love you guys! As they say here - Calabaza, calabaza cada quién a su casa
