Week 10 Update

By Hannah Friday, July 17, 2026 Built with: Hope City - El Paso, TX
Hey guys! Another week has flown by and wow the group this week was so awesome! We built two homes with this team from El Paso, Texas - Hope City Church! One group was with David, the President of Casas and the other group was with me and Erin! This is David's home church so it was really cool to get to know everyone in this group throughout the week. The team flew in on Sunday and we started building both homes on Monday and completed both on Wednesday. The family we built for this week was Alvaro (24) who is a farmer, his wife Sara (25), and their daughters Madison (7) and Marta (1). The Pastor sponsoring both of these families is Pastor Diego and he was so awesome to work with. Our team was able to pray over him during our dedication after praying over the home and it was such a beautiful moment. Most of the team we had this week spoke fluent Spanish and it was amazing getting to see the connections they built with the family and community in the short few days we were on site! Definitely inspiration for me to learn more Spanish! One of the women from our group, Theresa, prepared a Bible study for the kids in the community for each day we were building and by the third day she had 45 kids show up to hear her lessons - God is so good!! It was incredible to see how fast word spread for this little study she prepared and all the precious kids that were impacted this week. They ordered pizza the last day and sat on the porch eating the first meal in the home. The team was so eager to help and work - it was truly a pleasure getting to talk to them and work alongside them this week. We had some time after completing the home on Wednesday before the dedication so our crew decided to build a table and some shelves in the home for the family! We had some great photographers on the team this week and they captured some really cool moments. I am so grateful God allowed me to get to experience the love and eagerness to serve from the whole group this week. And getting to work with Erin was so amazing! She is truly inspiring to me and she has become such an amazing friend. I am feeling so blessed this week - I cannot believe it's only two weeks until I come back to the States. Thank you all for following along and for your prayers and encouragement. Love and miss you all!!
