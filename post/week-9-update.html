Week 9 Update

By Hannah Sunday, July 12, 2026
Hey guys! Well this past week has been pretty fun! Saturday night we set off fireworks & sparklers and celebrated the Fourth of July with hotdogs and Kelsey's homemade cookies. It was a really fun night! On Sunday we left for Lake Atitlan for our intern retreat. Usually the retreat happens at the end of the summer, but because the next 3 weeks will be so busy we had our retreat this past week. We got to hang out at the Lake and ride in a boat to one of the local towns to shop and eat. I've had a lot of time to reflect this week and grow closer to these awesome people that God has put in my path. Thank you Jesus! On Wednesday, we celebrated Kelsey's 19th birthday! On Thursday, we did some office work and scouted our sites for the next few weeks. Yesterday, we celebrated my birthday and Ana & Gabby got us a piñata! I am so grateful for these people and this experience. Please keep us all in your prayers these next few weeks in the craziness of building. Love and miss you all!
