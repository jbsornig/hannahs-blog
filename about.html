About This Mission

Hey guys! I will be spending 3 months in Guatemala on a mission trip building homes for local families. Each week I'll be helping coordinate the build between the mission groups and the families they serve. This trip is about more than building walls and roofs. It's about forming relationships, sharing the love of Jesus, and making a lasting difference in the lives of Guatemalan families who need a safe place to call home. Thank you for following along with me this summer!
